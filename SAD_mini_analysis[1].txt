
STUDENT PERFORMANCE PREDICTION SYSTEM
SYSTEM ANALYSIS AND DESIGN (SAD) MINI-ANALYSIS

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Academic risk is sometimes identified only after students receive
poor academic results. This prototype explores the use of historical
student information and academic indicators to identify students
who may be academically at risk.

2. USERS
- Faculty Member
- Academic Analyst

3. INPUTS
The system uses student attributes and academic indicators such as:
- Age
- Sex
- Study Time
- Absences
- Past Class Failures
- First Period Grade (G1)
- Second Period Grade (G2)
- Other available student attributes

4. PROCESS
1. Obtain the Student Performance Mathematics dataset.
2. Validate the dataset and required columns.
3. Preprocess numerical and categorical data.
4. Train a Random Forest classification model.
5. Evaluate the model using accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
6. Accept a CSV upload for student risk prediction.
7. Display prediction results.

5. OUTPUTS
- AT RISK prediction
- NOT AT RISK prediction
- Model evaluation summary
- Student risk distribution
- Downloadable prediction results

6. LIMITATION
The model is trained using a historical dataset from Portuguese
secondary schools. Its performance may differ when applied to
students from another institution or population. Predictions are
estimates for classroom demonstration and are not diagnoses.
They should not be the sole basis for real academic decisions.
